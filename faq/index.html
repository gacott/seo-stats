Frequently asked questions

How the reference is built, graded and licensed.

What is SEO Facts?

SEO Facts is an open reference of 97 search engine optimisation statistics. Each entry includes its source, publication date, sample size and a confidence grade.

How are statistics selected?

We include figures that are widely used in search practice and that can be traced to a stated source. We prioritise entries where the method and sample are disclosed.

What does the confidence grade mean?

High means a large sample, transparent method and independent reproduction. Medium means an adequate sample with some limitation. Low means a small sample, self-reported figures, or an undisclosed method.

Can I use these statistics in my own work?

Yes, under the CC BY 4.0 licence, with attribution. Each entry has a permalink you can cite directly.

How often is the reference updated?

Monthly. Corrections are logged on the changelog rather than replacing figures silently.

Who funds SEO Facts?

The reference is self-funded by the research collective and does not accept sponsorship from vendors whose data appears in it.

Why do some figures differ from other sources?

Different studies use different samples, time windows and definitions. We report the source and its method so you can see why two numbers differ.

How do I report an error?

Write to research@seofacts.org with the entry id and your evidence. We aim to respond within five working days.

Can I download the dataset?

Yes. A CSV and JSON export are available from the download links on the home page and in the footer of every page.

Do you track visitors?

The site counts page hits, including known crawlers, using a server-side pixel. It does not build advertising profiles.
