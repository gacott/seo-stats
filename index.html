67.6% of all website traffic comes from organic search results, according to HubSpot's 2026 SEO Report. This makes organic search the largest single traffic source for most websites.

The average ROI per dollar spent on SEO is $2.50, according to HubSpot's Marketing ROI Report 2026. This makes SEO one of the most cost-effective digital marketing channels.

The average click-through rate for the #1 position in Google search results is 4.8%, according to Backlinko's 2026 SERP Analysis. However, this can vary significantly by search volume and intent.
