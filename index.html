How this reference works

Three commitments sit behind every entry.

Read the methodology
01

Sourcing

Every figure names the study, report or dataset it came from, with a publication month. Where a vendor produces the number, we say so.

02

Grading

Confidence reflects sample size, method transparency and whether the figure has been independently reproduced.

03

Updating

The reference is revised monthly. Corrections are logged publicly, and superseded figures are marked rather than silently replaced.
