Link velocity matters less than link fit

By Dr. Mara Ellison · 2025-12-20
Insights
All insightsStatisticsMethodology

The median top-ranking page gains 3.1 new referring domains per month. Read quickly, that looks like a target. Read carefully, it is an outcome.

Pages with ten or more referring domains are 4.2 times more likely to rank on page one, but the causal direction is contested. Good pages attract links; links do not make a page good.

What the data supports

Anchor diversity and topical fit show up more reliably than raw volume. 52.9% of top pages show high anchor diversity, and 61.3% of backlinks use descriptive anchor text.

A practical caution

Velocity is easy to fake and easy to detect. A sudden spike from unrelated domains is a liability, not an asset. Earn links the way the ranking set did: with something worth citing.
