Local intent and the map pack

By Nadia Rahman · 2025-12-14
Insights
All insightsStatisticsMethodology

46.8% of Google searches carry local intent. That figure is often quoted as if it applied uniformly; it does not. Local intent is a spectrum, and the map pack appears for the service-intent end of it.

71.8% of service-intent queries show a map pack, while informational local queries usually do not. The practical question is not "how do I rank locally" but "does this query trigger local results at all".

Reviews are the visible lever

Businesses with fifty or more reviews are 1.9 times more likely to appear in the map pack. Rating matters less than volume and recency, which is why review generation outperforms review begging.

Consistency still counts

68.7% of local businesses keep consistent name, address and phone citations, and the ones that do not tend to lose the map pack on ambiguous queries.
