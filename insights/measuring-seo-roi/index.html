Measuring SEO ROI without fooling yourself

By Dr. Mara Ellison · 2025-12-02
Insights
All insightsStatisticsMethodology

The reference puts the average return on SEO at $2.50 per dollar spent. Like any average, it hides more than it reveals.

Organic search produces 3.5 times more leads than paid per dollar in the accounts we track, and the median payback period sits at 7.8 months. That payback horizon is why SEO is chronically under-funded: it looks bad in a quarterly review and excellent over a year.

Attribute honestly

Most inflated SEO ROI numbers come from attribution, not performance. If organic touches a journey that paid also touches, assigning the full value to organic double-counts. Use a consistent model and state it.

Measure the counterfactual

The honest question is not "what did organic return" but "what would have happened without it". That is harder to answer, and that is exactly why it is worth doing.
