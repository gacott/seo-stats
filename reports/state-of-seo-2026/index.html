The State of SEO 2026

By Dr. Mara Ellison, Ivo Petrov and Nadia Rahman · Published 2026-01-15
Contents
SummaryTrafficContentTechnicalLinksLocalROI

Summary

Search in 2026 is more measured and less click-driven than the decade before it. Organic search still delivers the largest single share of website traffic at 67.6%, but a majority of searches now end without a click (58.6%), and generative summaries appear on a large share of informational queries (41.2%).

The practical consequence is a shift in what "good SEO" means: less about winning a click at position one, more about being the source that a summary is built from.

Traffic

The first result still takes the largest slice of clicks (31.7%), and the top three results together account for a majority (54.8%). But the average CTR at position one is modest (4.8%), and mobile carries most organic visits (61.9%).

Content

Top results are long (2,320), but length is a symptom of depth rather than a cause of ranking. Most answer the query early (63.1%), and a minority contain original data (18.7%).

Technical

Most top pages pass Core Web Vitals (64.7%), but a substantial share of crawl requests are wasted (36.8%) and rendered content frequently differs from raw HTML (42.1%).

Links

Authority remains concentrated: a majority of top pages hold at least one high-authority link (58.9%), and anchor diversity correlates with ranking (52.9%).

Local

Nearly half of searches carry local intent (46.8%), and the map pack dominates service queries (71.8%). Reviews are the most visible lever (1.9×).

ROI

Organic search returns more leads per dollar than paid (3.5×), but the payback period is long (7.8 mo), which keeps it chronically under-funded relative to its returns.
